`capstone_workflow.py` is the Chapter14 import-safe local experiment. It defines the educational binary target quality>=6 from the source sensory score, hashes all 11 chemistry features excluding quality into duplicate-profile groups and creates disjoint fit/CV (966 rows/815 groups), decision-validation (313/272) and final-test (320/272) roles. Profiles are proxies, not verified bottle/batch identities. There are 1,599 observations and 1,359 unique chemistry profiles. Source labels are subjective; the binary cutoff is a modeling choice, not objective grading truth.

Three fit-only grouped stratified folds compare six fixed learned candidates and one prior baseline. Highest mean AP selects the model, exact ties use declared order. Fit-only StandardScaler is inside each applicable pipeline. Decision validation selects a threshold from .05 through .95 in .05 increments under illustrative FP1/FN4 costs; these are not measured business costs. The selected fit-role model is not silently refitted after policy selection. Only after these locks is final test evaluated. Results are now disclosed; repeating the deterministic audit is not fresh confirmation, and subsequent tuning needs new untouched evidence. A 300-resample profile-group bootstrap concerns fixed predictions/policy, assumes exchangeable groups and omits training/search uncertainty. Permutation/ablation diagnostics are associational and do not establish causal chemistry effects or deployment usefulness.
